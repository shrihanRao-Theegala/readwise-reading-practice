BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Three habits that help

Try these suggestions before starting your quiz.

🔎

Read for the big idea

After each paragraph, pause and ask: “What is the main point?” Avoid getting stuck on one unfamiliar word.

🧩

Use clues to infer

For questions about feelings or motives, combine what the passage says with what the character does. Do not guess without evidence.

📌

Find proof in the text

Before choosing an answer, look back for a sentence or detail that supports it. Cross out options that add unsupported information.
